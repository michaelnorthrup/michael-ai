How this site works

November 2026, 2 min read

The chat on this site is a small production AI application, so it gets the same treatment I'd give one at work: grounded answers, guardrails, and a hard ceiling on cost.

Architecture

  • One Cloudflare Worker serves the static page and a single /api/chat endpoint that streams answers from Claude.
  • Grounding: the model answers only from a written profile of my background. If something isn't in it, the assistant says so and points you to email instead of guessing.
  • Fit check: paste a job description and it maps requirements to evidence, then lists honest gaps. No scores, and instructions hidden inside a posting are ignored.

Guardrails

  • It won't discuss compensation or job-search status, and it won't invent employers, dates, or numbers.
  • Off-topic requests and attempts to override its instructions get a polite redirect.
  • It speaks about me in the third person and says plainly that it's an AI.

Cost controls

  • A global daily message cap, enforced exactly across all servers by a Durable Object.
  • A per-visitor rate limit, size limits on messages and history, and short answers by default.

Questions are logged anonymously, without IP addresses, so I can see what people ask and improve the answers.
