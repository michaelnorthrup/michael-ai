Selected work

Three projects, with the result first. Internal names are left out.

BambooHR · Payroll

An AI auditor that payroll experts can actually check

Passed its go/no-go review. The same method is rolling out in a second audit product.

Problem

Payroll mistakes are expensive and experts can't review everything. An AI that flags anomalies is useless if they can't see why.

My role

Wrote the spec, built the system, and worked it through rounds of feedback with payroll specialists.

Approach

An agent with evals from day one and human review of every finding, plus a lineage tool that traces each rule to its source data. Reused for document AI that checks new clients' tax filings.

BambooHR · Platform

Making the next AI app a folder, not weeks of tickets

Platform owner. First version live in staging, with the first apps moving on.

Problem

Every internal AI app rebuilt sign-in, access control, data and PII access, LLM access, and deployment, and waited weeks on the same approvals.

My role

Owner and architect. Audited the existing apps, wrote the plan and architecture decisions, ran the cross-team design session, and shipped the first version.

Approach

Pay for identity, governed data access, LLM access, and deployment once at the platform level, with fail-closed access by default.

BILL · Customer payments

Turning an overlooked decline problem into revenue

$25M payment-volume opportunity and a $3M monthly revenue increase.

Problem

Failed customer payments were costing revenue and hurting the payment experience, and the problem had little attention.

My role

Found it, built the business case, pitched it, and drove it end to end, from models to the production ML foundation.

Approach

Predicted half of transaction declines ahead of time so the business could step in, on a monitored data lake with drift detection and repeatable retraining.
