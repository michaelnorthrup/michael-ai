An AI auditor that payroll experts can actually check

Passed go/no-go review · method reused for a second audit product

Problem
Payroll mistakes are expensive and experts can't review everything. An AI that flags anomalies is useless if the experts can't see why.
My role
Wrote the spec, built the system, and worked it through rounds of feedback with payroll specialists.
Approach
An agent-based anomaly detector with evals from day one and human review of every finding.
